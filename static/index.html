fbdump v1.0

Facebook Video CDN Link Extractor

Paste a Facebook video, reel or post URL. Get the direct signed CDN MP4 links. Nothing is downloaded.

🔗
Try: share link reel permalink watch link

Progressive MP4 — video + audio

DASH — video only

Heads up: CDN links are signed (oh=/oe=) and expire in roughly 1–2 days. After that they return 403 — just run the extraction again to refresh them. Progressive links include an AAC audio track; DASH links are video-only.
